# PLAN-analytics — review of mvd-analytics core

> Updated 2026-07-05 against main @ 05e2ed9 (schema v47). Originally written against a pre-#98 tree; findings re-verified after merging #96–#102.

Overall the core pipeline is in good shape: the three-phase design (event pass → core/derived Finalize with `CoreOutputs` → ordered post-processors) is clean, ordering constraints are documented at the registration site, the schema contract in `result/` is exceptionally well documented, and the `view/` package's row/columnar parity discipline (shared reducers, parity tests, justified fast paths) is genuinely strong engineering. The problems cluster in three areas: (1) a family of "forgot one field" bugs where a Result field added in one place is missed by the post-processors that rewrite times/teams — `KillEvents` is demonstrably shipped on the wrong clock and with pre-normalize team labels today; (2) fragile string matching around match timing and obituaries, including one unreachable obituary pattern and a chat line being able to start/end the match; and (3) avoidable duplication — the obituary parser exists twice with semantic drift, the `fullserverinfo` parser exists three times, and several small helpers are reimplemented per file. There is also one confirmed piece of dead code (`tracks.go`) whose internal staleness proves it is unmaintained.

## Big picture

- **A1 — The "every time-stamped field" invariant is enforced only by memory.** `normalizeMatchRelativeTimes` (analyzer/postprocess.go:27) and `normalizeDuelTeams` (analyzer/duel_normalize.go:37, wrapped by `duelTeamNormalize`) must each enumerate every timestamped/team-labelled field on the Result. Nothing checks completeness, and both already missed `TimelineAnalysis.KillEvents` (see F1). #100 grew the duel enumeration again (Items phases, WeaponPickups, Backpacks, Shots — duel_normalize.go:219–314), which proves the list keeps growing and is still maintained purely by memory. Suggest a cheap structural invariant test run over the golden corpus: every `TimelineAnalysis` event stream's timestamps must lie in `[−demoOffset, Global.MatchEnd + slack]` and, post-duel-normalize on a duel demo, every team label must be a player name. That converts "remember to edit two post-processors" into a failing test.

- **A2 — Obituary parsing is implemented twice and has drifted.** `frag.go` (`parseObituary` and its sub-checkers, frag.go:337–800) and `messages.go` (`parseObituarySimple`, messages.go:187–441) carry near-identical pattern tables (~250 duplicated lines) that already disagree (see F4). One parser should produce a neutral parsed-obituary struct; frag and messages should both consume it, differing only in output mapping and team lookup.

- **A3 — Slot→identity resolution is re-implemented per analyzer.** The same fallback chain (CoreOutputs `SlotIdentityAt` → `ctx.Players[slot]` → NameTable team backfill) appears in `TimelineAnalyzer.resolveAt` (timeline.go:401), `DamageAnalyzer.resolveAt` (damage.go:217), `WeaponPickupsAnalyzer.identityAt` (weapon_pickups.go:92), `FragAnalyzer.resolveDeathName` (frag.go:299), and inline in `ItemAnalyzer.resolveAttributions` (items.go:1311–1322). Each copy differs subtly (some backfill team from the name table, some don't), which is exactly how the next reconnect bug gets introduced. Move one canonical `ResolveSlotAt(co, players, slot, tMs) SlotInfo` next to `CoreOutputs` and delete the copies.

- **A4 — Errors are swallowed at two pipeline boundaries.** Finalize errors are correctly captured into `Result.Errors` (registry.go:254), but (a) a mid-demo `source.Next()` error silently truncates the event pass — the comment says "Log and stop" but nothing logs or records (registry.go:200–208) — and (b) `regionControlPost` discards the `view.RegionControl` error (postprocess.go:489–491). Both should append to `Result.Errors` so a consumer can tell a clean parse from a truncated one.

- **A5 — Adding a PositionTrack column costs ~7 hand-edited sites.** `streamBuilder` parallel arrays (state.go:82–93), `recordPosition` (timeline_streams.go:104), `appendSlice` (timeline_streams.go:370–410), `toPlayerStream` (timeline_streams.go:239–271), `shiftAndFilterPosition` (postprocess.go:291–333), `PositionTrack.MarshalJSON` (result/coord.go:71–91), plus the view-layer kind/slice/columnar plumbing. The v31/v32/v33 history shows each new column touched all of them. Full abstraction may not pay for itself, but at minimum the "keep in sync" checklist that already exists in coord.go should be one canonical comment listing all sites, referenced from each.

- **A6 — view/ is the healthiest part of the reviewed surface.** Deliberate fast paths with parity tests (`fastReduce`, `TestFastReduceParity`), a coherent field/reducer vocabulary, and clear package-boundary doctrine (reducers.go package comment). Findings there are limited to small duplications (F12, nits).

## Findings

- **F1 — `KillEvents` is emitted demo-relative and with pre-normalize team labels (missed by two post-processors).** analyzer/timeline_finalize.go:132–146 builds `KillEvents` from `FragEntry.Time` (demo-relative int32 ms); `normalizeMatchRelativeTimes` shifts `FragEvents`/`DeathEvents`/`PowerupEvents`/`FragStreaks` but not `KillEvents` (postprocess.go:40–55), and `normalizeDuelTeams` rewrites teams on FragEvents/DeathEvents/FragStreaks/PowerupEvents but not KillEvents (duel_normalize.go:182–205). Verified against goldens: in `1on1_bananfalco_betowen_240426_dm2.json`, `killEvents[0].time`=36130 vs the matching `deathEvents[0].time`=25844 with `demoOffset`=10286 (exactly the offset), and `killEvents[0].team`="red" while every other stream says "banan falco"/"betowen". Re-verified at v47: the regenerated goldens still show the identical discrepancy, and neither #100 (which extended the duel rewrite to pickups/shots) nor #101 (which re-gated fragEvents/deathEvents on name) touched KillEvents in either post-processor. RESULT_SCHEMA.md claims killEvents is "parallel to DeathEvents", and the web drill-down plots `killEvents − deathEvents` on one axis, so every kill is displaced ~10 s late and duel team colors are wrong. Fix: shift `ta.KillEvents[i].Time` in `normalizeMatchRelativeTimes` and rewrite `ke.Team` in `duelTeamNormalize`; regenerate goldens; add the A1 invariant test. **(correctness)**

- **F2 — Chat lines can start or end the match: `MatchTimingDetector.OnPrint` ignores print level.** matchtiming.go:52–74 lowercases every print and matches substrings like `"go!"`, `"game over"`, `"timelimit hit"`; all nine embedding analyzers feed it every `PrintEvent` unfiltered (e.g. timeline.go:188, frag.go:80; shots.go:152 joined the list at v39). A pre-match chat line "name: go go go!" (PRINT_CHAT, level 3) flips `Started` and starts recording warmup state; a mid-match "gg game over" chat flips `Ended` and freezes every stat/stream for the rest of the demo. Real match-start/end prints are broadcast bprints at level ≤ 2. Fix: `if e.Level == events.PrintChat { return }` at the top of `OnPrint`. Related: `FragAnalyzer.handleObituaryPrint` accepts level 3 too (frag.go:110, "to be safe"), so a chat message containing " rides " can inject a fake frag — tighten to `<= 2` unless there is corpus evidence of level-3 obituaries (messages.go already branches chat away before obituary parsing, messages.go:60–75, so it does this correctly). **(correctness)**

- **F3 — CRMod SSG obituary pattern is unreachable; those kills are mislabeled `gl` with killer "2 scoops of X".** In `checkKill`'s ordered pattern list, the generic `{" eats ", "gl"}` (frag.go:481) precedes the CRMod `{" eats 2 scoops of ", "ssg"}` (frag.go:522). For "V eats 2 scoops of K's lead shot", `strings.Index` hits `" eats "` first, so the entry at :522 can never match; weapon becomes "gl" and `extractKillerName("2 scoops of K's lead shot")` trims at the `'s lead shot` suffix and returns "2 scoops of K" — a phantom player name that pollutes `byPlayer` and every name join. Fix: order the more specific pattern first (or match `" eats "` only when followed by a `'s pineapple`/`'s grenade` suffix). `messages.go` lacks the CRMod entries entirely, so it mislabels the same line — folding into the shared parser (A2/F4) fixes both. **(correctness)**

- **F4 — `parseObituarySimple` duplicates frag.go's tables with semantic drift.** messages.go:187–441 vs frag.go:337–800. Confirmed drift: drowning weapon code is `"water"` in frag.go:395–396 but `"drown"` in messages.go:390–391; frag.go handles `" somehow becomes bored with life"` before the shorter substring (frag.go:380–381) while messages.go only has the short form (messages.go:382) so the "somehow" variant is mislabeled `rl` there; frag.go's CRMod (frag.go:521–526) and k_spawnicide (frag.go:430–432) patterns are absent from messages.go; the gibbed-by suffix check differs (`HasSuffix` frag.go:739 vs `Contains` messages.go:363). Each new obituary discovered in a demo must currently be added twice, and evidently isn't. Fix per A2: single table + single walker returning `{killer, victim, weapon, flags}`; messages maps that to `MatchEvent`, frag to `FragEntry`. Saves ~200 lines and eliminates the drift class. **(correctness drift / bloat)**

- **F5 — `isDuelResult` can rewrite a team game as a duel.** duel_normalize.go:323–334: the DemoInfo check only short-circuits on *true*; when DemoInfo lists 4 players the function falls through to `len(result.Match.Players) == 2`. Because MatchAnalyzer drops 0-frag players (F6), a 2on2 in which two players finish on exactly 0 frags yields `Match.Players == 2` and the entire result gets the duel team rewrite (teams renamed to player names, Match.Players rebuilt from DemoInfo). Fix: when DemoInfo is present, make it authoritative — `if result.DemoInfo != nil { return len(result.DemoInfo.Players) == 2 }` — and only fall back to Match.Players when there is no demoinfo. **(correctness)**

- **F6 — MatchAnalyzer silently drops players with exactly 0 frags.** match.go:106–110 (`if stat.Frags == 0 { continue }`, comment "likely joined briefly but didn't play"). A player who legitimately finishes on 0 (e.g. kills cancelled by suicides) vanishes from `Match.Players` and `Match.Teams` aggregation, and the filter feeds F5. This is exactly the kind of "looks wrong, drop it" filter CLAUDE.md's surface-authoritative-data policy warns about, and the justification comment is a guess, not an invariant. Suggest removing the filter (or gating on a real signal such as "no spawn/position/frag-update ever seen for the slot") and regenerating goldens. **(correctness / policy)**

- **F7 — `fullserverinfo` parsing is triplicated.** `MetadataAnalyzer.parseFullserverinfo` (metadata.go:92–113), `ItemAnalyzer.extractMapName` (items.go:310–328), and `BackpackAnalyzer.extractMapName` (backpacks.go:120–138) are byte-identical quote-strip + `\\`-split walkers. There is also a *different* package-level `extractMapName(levelName string)` in match.go:170 — same name, unrelated job. Fix: one package helper `parseInfoString(cmd string) map[string]string` (or `serverinfoValue(cmd, key)`); items/backpacks call it for `"map"`; rename one of the two `extractMapName`s. **(bloat / readability)**

- **F8 — tracks.go is dead code with a stale-schema bug inside.** `ExtractTracks`/`TracksResult` have zero callers outside the file (grep confirms), the header says "shelved scaffolding … no production callers today" (tracks.go:36–49), and it contains a live bug proving the rot: tracks.go:86 computes `matchEndMs := int32(result.Streams.Global.MatchEnd * 1000)` under a comment claiming MatchEnd is "float64 seconds on the public schema" — `GlobalStream.MatchEnd` has been int32 ms since schema v8 (result/streams.go:219), so the value is 1000× too large (and overflows int32 past ~35 min). Delete tracks.go + tracks.md (git preserves them for revival), or fix the unit and register the analyzer; keeping a known-buggy shelf item invites accidental reuse. **(bloat / correctness of latent code)**

- **F9 — Source read errors vanish; region-control errors vanish.** registry.go:200–208: on `source.Next()` error the loop breaks with neither logging nor a `Result.Errors` entry, so a demo truncated by a decode error is indistinguishable from a clean one (the comment "Log and stop; partial results still usable" describes logging that does not exist). postprocess.go:489–491: `rc, err := view.RegionControl(...); if err != nil || rc == nil { return }` likewise discards the error. Fix: `result.Errors = append(result.Errors, "event stream aborted: "+err.Error())` (requires moving the Result construction above the loop or stashing the error), and the analogous append in `regionControlPost`. **(correctness / error handling)**

- **F10 — timeline_streams.go hand-rolls ~120 lines of mechanical repetition.** (a) Seven identical dedup-append methods `recordHealth/recordArmor/recordLoc/recordShells/recordNails/recordRockets/recordCells` (timeline_streams.go:42–96) — one `appendChange(col *[]changeI16, tMs int32, v int16)` helper collapses them. (b) `toPlayerStream` repeats the same convert-loop for seven ChangeI16 streams (timeline_streams.go:183–238) — one `toChangeI16s([]changeI16) []result.ChangeI16` helper. (c) `intToStr` (timeline_streams.go:303–324) is a 22-line reimplementation of `strconv.Itoa`, used at :298, :586 and identity.go:295 — replace with `strconv.Itoa`. Net ~100 LOC saved with zero behavior change. **(bloat)**

- **F11 — `shiftAndFilterChangeI16` / `shiftAndFilterChangeStr` are copy-paste twins.** postprocess.go:182–210 vs :212–238 differ only in element type. Either a small generic (`func shiftAndFilterChanges[C any](stream []C, getT func(C) int32, mk func(int32, C) C, matchStartMs int32) []C`) or making `result.ChangeI16`/`ChangeStr` instantiations of a generic `Change[V]` (JSON shape unchanged) removes one copy. Low urgency, but this is the file every new stream type must extend (A1), so halving the pattern helps. **(bloat)**

- **F12 — Exact duplicate interval-membership helper inside `view`.** `intervalContains` (view/buckets.go:836–843) and `intervalsOverlapAt` (view/region_control.go:481–488) are the same function (`[]result.Interval`, `int32` → bool, half-open test). Delete one, keep one name. **(bloat)**

- **F13 — Two different close-at-match-end passes for powerup intervals, with divergent fallbacks.** `detectPowerupEvents` closes quad/pent/ring per player using `timing.EndTime` or *that player's* last position sample (timeline_powerups.go:24–32), then `buildStreamsResult` later calls `streams.finalize(matchEndMs)` where the fallback is the *global* max position sample (timeline_finalize.go:277–290). On a demo cut before intermission the two passes use different end times for the same intervals (the earlier close wins because `closeAtMatchEnd` is idempotent, so the Streams output inherits the per-player value while weapons get the global one). Compute the effective match-end once in `Finalize` and pass it to both. **(readability / correctness edge)**

- **F14 — `processSyntheticRespawns` runs (and allocates) on every event.** items.go:306 calls it from `OnEvent` for *every* event; it then builds and sorts `sortedKeys(a.syntheticChain)` (items.go:497) each time. On a 4on4 demo that is hundreds of thousands of sort+alloc calls for a map that changes rarely. Fix: keep a `nextDue float64` (min of predicted+settle) and early-return when `currentT < nextDue` or the chain is empty; recompute `nextDue` only when the chain mutates. **(perf, cheap fix)**

- **F15 — Columnar position/view/velocity builders are triplicated.** `buildPositionCols` / `buildViewCols` / `buildVelocityCols` (view/columnar.go:398–503) are the same loop parameterized by (field code, output keys, tuple extractor). One helper taking `field string, keys []string, put func(v any, j int) bool` removes ~60 lines and means the next multi-column field (e.g. a future acceleration) is data, not a fourth copy. **(bloat / extensibility)**

- **F16 — Dead return value with a misleading comment.** `resolveLocsAndFilterBlips` returns `(locTable, locIndex)`; the only caller discards the index with `_ = locIndex // used by the regions builder below if regions are configured` (timeline_finalize.go:198, :214) — `buildControlRegions` does not take it and no other consumer exists. Drop the second return and the comment. **(bloat / readability)**

## Low priority / nits

- view/stateat.go:263–281 `nearestPositionIndex` linear-scans the whole time-sorted track; use `sort.Search` like `nearestSampleIndex` (analyzer/airgibs.go:171) — which is itself a third copy of the "nearest sample to t" idea alongside `positionAt` (analyzer/teamkill_telefrag.go:171).
- registry.go:272–279 the post-processor ordering comment omits `deriveDemoStartAnchor`, `aimPost`, and `airgibsPost`, all of which are registered (registry.go:342, :347, :348) — update the list, ordering comments are load-bearing here (the gap got wider when #97 added aimPost).
- result/streams.go:236–237 stale doc: "Indices align across the five arrays. Coordinates are int32 …" contradicts the actual `[]float32` columns (v33) and the twelve-column reality; fix the sentence.
- weapon_pickups.go:440–442 stale comment: "ctx.Players[slot].Name (patched by the registry …)" describes the pre-CoreOutputs mechanism that was removed; the code actually uses `identityAt`.
- timeline_finalize.go:259–260 comment says "top 5 longest frag streaks" but calls `detectFragStreaks(10, …)`.
- Determinism ties: `detectPowerupEvents` iterates `a.playerState` (map) and sorts only by `Time` with non-stable `sort.Slice` (timeline_powerups.go:16, :44) — same-ms events order randomly; `appendIntervalEvents` iterates a map of streams (view/events.go:354) with a final `(T, Type)` sort that leaves same-T/same-Type ties unordered. Both are one `sort.SliceStable`/tie-break away from byte-stable output, which the rest of the code works hard to guarantee.
- `disambiguatePlayerName` suffixes *all* colliding names (timeline_streams.go:296–301), not just "the later one" as the buildStreamsResult doc says (:524); and two same-name identities sharing the same `repSlot` (slot reuse) would still collide after suffixing — tie-break on identity key if it ever matters.
- registry.go:214 indexes `ctx.Players[e.Player.Slot]` without a bounds check while identity.go:109 defensively checks the same slot — either trust the parser in both places or guard in both.
- `intervalState.updateInterval` (timeline_streams.go:126–140): the inner `if s.held` on the false branch is provably always true; simplify.
- Two union-find implementations: identity.go:352–375 (type) and timeline_regions.go:215–231 (inline closures); share the small type.
- **[RESOLVED on main — #100]** `duelTeamNormalize` also left `WeaponPickups[].Team/DropperTeam` and `Backpacks[].Team` with raw pre-normalize team strings — #100 added the rewrite for Items phases, WeaponPickups (incl. DropperTeam) and Backpacks (duel_normalize.go:219–254), plus Shots/ByPlayer team labels and victim-kind reclassification (:256–314). The A1 completeness concern still applies to future fields.
- items.go:1338–1340 `containsKind` is a one-line wrapper over `slices.Contains`; call it directly.
- `LegacyReducerSet` (view/fields.go:177) is an alias with one caller (mvd-web WASM) — fine to keep, but the "preserves the option to diverge later" rationale is the kind of speculative hook the codebase otherwise avoids.
- diagnostic/diagnostic_test.go only `t.Logf`s quality warnings and never fails; that is documented harness behavior, but consider promoting a few cheap invariants (e.g. "no health > 250 in streams", the F1 time-bounds check) to hard assertions so regressions gate CI.

## New since this review (v44–v47, not covered above)

Surface merged after this review was written; listed for completeness, not deep-reviewed.

- **Shots/aim pipeline hardening (#98, v44; #99, v45).** `analyzer/aim.go` now attributes a hit shot's crosshair samples to the server-confirmed `Shot.Victims` instead of the nearest live enemy, and `analyzer/shots.go` classifies each victim enemy/team/self (`Shot.VictimKinds`, `WeaponShots` enemy/team/self hit buckets, `result/sample.go` team columns). Not reviewed here — but note the A1 pattern held: `Shots.Shots[].Time` *is* enumerated in `normalizeMatchRelativeTimes` (postprocess.go:172–176) and Shots team labels/victim kinds in `normalizeDuelTeams` (#100), while `Aim` dodges both by being computed post-normalize (`aimPost`, registered after `duelTeamNormalize`, registry.go:347).
- **Weapon-stay pickup recovery (#100, v46).** New `analyzer/weaponstay.go` (mode detection from serverinfo) + STAT_ITEMS flip synthesis in items.go / weapon_pickups.go; `WeaponPickup` gains `Inferred` and `Source: "unknown"` (result/weapon_pickups.go). No new timestamped fields — nothing new for the F1/A1 enumerations to miss.
- **Duel pickup/shot normalization (#100).** duel_normalize.go:219–314 — resolves the old duelTeamNormalize nit (above); also flips duel `VictimKinds` "team"→"enemy" and folds `TeamHits` into `EnemyHits`.
- **Frag-streak opening life + name-gated timeline events (#101).** `timeline_streaks.go` synthesizes a match-start spawn so the opening life can carry a streak; timeline_finalize.go now gates fragEvents/deathEvents on a resolvable *name* rather than team (adopting killEvents' rationale — its commit message documents an audit of the other team-keyed consumers for the same loss class).
- **LG whiff reclassification (#102, v47).** aim.go replaces the endpoint-only "blocked" heuristic with beam-line hull-intersection tests (`blocked`/`outOfRange` require a live enemy on the extended beam); `nearMiss` removed. Post-normalize like the rest of Aim, so no enumeration exposure.
- **Recurring pattern to watch:** the default post-processor chain now has nine entries (aimPost landed with #97) and the registry ordering comment did not keep up (nit above); every one of #98–#102 also required regenerating goldens, reinforcing the case for the A1 structural invariant test over per-PR memory.
